SCARA_F103 v0.29.9 GRBL-SCARA validation flow

Safety:
1. Keep the mechanism clear and disconnect laser power during initial checks.
2. Disconnect motor-driver power before flashing; firmware enables ENA on boot.
3. Software pulse closure does not prove that the mechanism did not lose steps.

Stage 1 - offline:
  cmake --build --preset Debug
  powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\verify_project.ps1
  C:\Users\22602\.conda\envs\Robot\python.exe ..\SCARA_UI\tests\sender_strategy_check.py
  C:\Users\22602\.conda\envs\Robot\python.exe ..\SCARA_UI\tests\sender_benchmark_check.py
  C:\Users\22602\.conda\envs\Robot\python.exe ..\SCARA_UI\tests\trajectory_planner_check.py
  C:\Users\22602\.conda\envs\Robot\python.exe ..\SCARA_UI\tests\ui_home_gate_check.py

Expected offline result:
  Flash <= 61 KB
  RAM leaves at least 4 KB
  100000-command sender benchmark peak_pending=64
  Text/handwriting short-block planner capacity=48 with at least 12 blocks prefetched when available

Stage 2 - flash with motor and laser power disconnected:
1. Confirm VERSION reports 0.29.9-grbl-scara.
2. Send `?`; require `<Idle|MPos:...|JPos:...|FS:...|Bf:...|Q:...|E:...|Seg:...|Pf:...|Rl:...|Pg:...|H:...|HS:...|A1:...|A2:...|Lz:...>`.
3. Burst mixed G0/G1/G2/G3 while polling `?`; require no RX overflow.
   During a 100000-line stream require `Pf` and segment underrun to remain
   unchanged; `Rl` may increase, and `Pg` must remain below the buffered margin.
4. Verify `$H` does not return `ok` until status reaches `HS:Done`.
5. Verify `!`, `~`, Ctrl-X and Jog Cancel 0x85 bypass the ordinary RX stream.

Stage 3 - oscilloscope:
1. Verify TIM1/TIM4 STEP pulses are 3 us active-low with at least 3 us recovery.
2. Verify DIR setup is at least 6 us before the next STEP.
3. Verify acceleration/deceleration event spacing is continuous.
4. Verify laser PWM is absent while idle, rapid, stopped, held or faulted.

Stage 4 - motion:
1. Home, then run low-speed short G1 and G2/G3 moves.
2. Run at 20 mm/s and 100 mm/s^2, then repeat at the intended high speed.
3. Run at least 30 left/right 30 mm exact-stop jog round trips.
4. Run handwriting and text outlines with at least 30 stroke transitions;
   require each G4 barrier to hold its stroke endpoint, with no JPos rewind and
   no increase in the Seg underrun counter. Require every input sharp corner to
   appear as a true G1 endpoint with no deleted point or generated corner arc.
5. Run a mixed line/arc car outline. Require every declared car-body point to
   appear as an exact G1 endpoint, while the two wheel curves remain true G2/G3
   arcs and line-to-arc transitions do not visibly pause.
6. Require software pulse endpoint closure, no accumulated physical drift, no
   segment underrun, and physical return error <= 0.3 mm.
