SCARA_F103 v0.26.3 recommended validation flow

Safety:
1. Keep the mechanism clear.
2. Disconnect motor-driver power before flashing. The firmware enables ENA on boot.
3. Do not treat MCU P:/M: status as physical position feedback. It is derived from
   commanded software pulse counts and cannot detect lost steps.

Stage 1 - offline checks:
  cmake --build --preset Debug
  powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\verify_project.ps1
  powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\simulate_binary_interpolator.ps1 -Count 3000

Stage 2 - flash with motor-driver power disconnected:
  powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\flash_and_verify_buffered.ps1 -Port AUTO -ConfirmMotorPowerOff

Confirm that VERSION reports 0.26.3 before motion testing.

Stage 3 - no-motion communication checks:
  powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\serial_link_check.ps1 -Port AUTO -Repeat 2
  powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\buffered_stream_capability_check.ps1 -Port AUTO
  powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\gcode_burst_no_motion_check.ps1 -Port AUTO -BurstLines 6
  powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\binary_joint_traj_stress.ps1 -Port AUTO -Count 1000 -HostTimed -TimedTicks 100 -ZeroBeforeRun -EnableMotion

Expected idle status:
  E:0
  Q:0
  Sq:0,4
  Hz:10000
  IC:<7200

Recorded v0.26.3 disconnected-motor baseline on COM12:
  idle IC=682
  1000-point HOST_TIMED IC=3907, underrun=0, max_gap=1, min_buffer=92
  final software pulse endpoint=(0,800)

Stage 4 - low-speed motion:
1. Restore motor-driver power and verify the mechanism is near its known home pose.
2. Run homing and confirm both switches behave correctly.
3. Start with a short line or jog at low speed.
4. Confirm binary diagnostics remain:
   underrun_ticks=0
   max_dispatch_gap_ticks<=1
   min_buffer>=4
   IC<7200

Stage 5 - repeat-position measurement:
1. Attach a dial indicator, camera fiducial, or external encoder at the test pose.
2. Run at least 30 A-B-A round trips at low speed, then repeat at normal speed.
3. Record the physical A-position error after every return.
4. Compare max, mean, and RMS physical error before and after v0.26.3.

Only physical measurement can prove repeat-position improvement. MCU software
position returning to the same pulse count proves command consistency, not that
the mechanism followed every pulse.
